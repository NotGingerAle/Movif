Our Story

A Legacy Stitched with Passion

Founded in 1992 at 1540, Sangtrashan Bazar, Paharganj — Movif India International began with a simple belief: that Indian garment craftsmanship deserves a global stage.

Today, as a proud second-generation family business, we carry that legacy forward with the same values: quality, reliability, and a personal touch that large factories can't replicate. We are importers, exporters, and manufacturers of ready-made garments for all ages and sizes.

We offer true end-to-end supply — from helping you select the right fabric, to ensuring your shipment arrives at your door, perfectly packed.
